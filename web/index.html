Stainless Craft, made with Three.js. Every texture is painted in code.

Piano: Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm (CC BY 3.0).

Footsteps, digging and placing: Minetest Game contributors (CC BY-SA 3.0, CC BY 3.0, CC0).

Thunder: hantorio, juskiddink, IllusiaProductions on Freesound, via the Minetest lightning mod (CC BY-SA).

Rain: Arctura (CC BY 3.0) and Q.K. (CC0). Wind: InspectorJ (CC BY 3.0). Via the regional_weather mod.

Crickets, owl: Mike Koenig (CC BY 3.0). Birds: reinsamba, PsychoBird (CC BY), UncleSigmund (CC0). Water drops: kddekadenz (CC0). Via the Minetest ambience mod.

Animals and hits: Freesound recordings via the Minetest mobs_animal and mobs_redo mods (CC0).

Tools breaking: HerbertBoland (CC BY 3.0). Furnace fire: Dynamicell (CC BY 3.0). Via Minetest Game.
