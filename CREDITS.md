All sounds in `sounds/` (bundled into `web/sounds.js` by `tools/pack_sounds.py`) are real recordings under
free licenses. For the game they were trimmed, converted to mono where noted, levelled, and the wind was
made into a seamless loop. Those edited versions keep the license of their original.
